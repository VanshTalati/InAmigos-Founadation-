Research notes – InAmigos Foundation
Checked: 22 November 2026

Primary sources:
1. Official homepage: https://inamigosfoundation.org.in/
   Lists the foundation's background, six initiatives, homepage figures, events and contact details.
2. Official About Us: https://inamigosfoundation.org.in/page/About-Us
   Describes the six projects and includes project-specific impact claims.
3. Official blog: https://inamigosfoundation.org.in/blog
   Contains articles about initiatives including Vikas, Udaan, Jeev and Bachpanshala.
4. Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/inamigosiaf/
5. LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/company/inamigos-foundation/

Content handling:
- Project summaries in the webpage are paraphrases, not copied campaign posts.
- Homepage figures (200 volunteers, 28 states, 6 causes, 50,000 beneficiaries) are attributed
  as figures published by the foundation. Confirm dates and methodology with the NGO.
- About page claims such as meals/clothing distributed, saplings planted, interns trained
  and animals fed daily should not be mixed with homepage figures without confirming the
  reporting period and definitions.
- Event examples displayed are dated 2025 and are clearly presented as past events.
- Use of photographs from the official website/social accounts requires permission or
  confirmation that reuse is allowed. Placeholder blocks are provided until approved
  images are supplied.
- Verify the volunteer form URL before publishing. Do not publish placeholder URLs.
- The official homepage crawl showed unrelated spam-style outbound links in its page
  content. This should be flagged to the internship coordinator/site administrator.
